# Code of Conduct

imchips seeks an inclusive, professional and respectful environment.

## Expected behavior

Be constructive, considerate and patient. Discuss ideas rather than attacking people. Respect privacy, personal boundaries and different skill levels. Report problems with reproducible evidence when possible.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, intimidation, hate speech, threats, sexualized misconduct, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and repeated unwelcome contact are not permitted.

## Reporting and enforcement

Contact the maintainer **Rheehose** privately through an available contact method on [the GitHub profile](https://github.com/hslcrb). Do not publish sensitive conduct reports in public issues. The maintainer may remove content, close discussions, warn or restrict participants when needed. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical; there is no guaranteed response-time SLA.

This policy applies to imchips issues, pull requests, discussions and other official project spaces.
